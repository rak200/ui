ui-card — pointer, entrance, exit

The pointer's effects come by kind — movement, shadow, fill, border — and each kind is a property of its own, so one of each can be chosen at once.

The card's surface is its host, which is where the limit S8 measured shows: an effect that moves the whole card cannot see a choice made on the card itself. Choose one of those for every ui-card or this instance and nothing happens — the page and the region still reach it.

A card cannot hold off its own removal, so its exit needs a way out the component owns, as the dialog owns its close. The buttons below take a card away that way, the exit first; choosing an exit plays it on every card and brings them back.
