Glass: translucent raised surfaces, over a page that is not flat

Question 2's recommendation. The raised surfaces — the cards, the toasts, the tip and the dialog — read a translucent colour and a backdrop filter; the surface itself stays opaque, so the fields inside a card are as legible as anywhere else. The theme also moves two grounds: a neutral slate accent, and the text in pure black and pure white. The page behind follows the scheme, as a host's would.

Why the reference looks clearer. It lets 94% of the page through where this lets 35 to 50% through, and it can, because it does not hold 4.5:1: its white text is 17:1 over its dark ground and 2.0:1 where a green blob passes under it. Try The reference's look below in the dark scheme — the closest to it, light text over a faint tint — then read the last rows under the panel.

The first of them holds for any page: the text over the glass composited on black and on white, which bound every backdrop there can be. With the reference's page behind, a second row holds for that page's colours only — what a theme could promise if it constrained the page behind it instead.
