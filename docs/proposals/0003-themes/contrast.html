High contrast: the reader's setting, not a theme

Question 4's recommendation. When the reader's system asks for more contrast, the default palette goes black and white: the text, the surface, the accent and the focus ring move to the poles, and the dark scheme sits on pure black. The border and the muted text follow on their own, because both mix toward the text. The outcome colours keep their hue — an error still says so in red.

The states are written out, not derived. Over pure black an 8% mix is #020202, 1.01:1 — the hover S5 found vanishing — and a black or white accent has no shade to move away from its label toward. A palette that is only black and white is fully known, so contrast.css gives its hover, pressed and striped greys as values, and says beside them what that costs a theme.

The panel is a stand-in: it carries the declarations the media query in contrast.css does, so the answer shows without the reader's setting.
